History

Cascade Current began with two friends, one borrowed raft, and a belief that the best adventures are shared. Since our first trip, we have welcomed families, beginners, and experienced paddlers to the river while caring for the wild places that make every journey special.

Today, our local guides combine technical river knowledge with warm hospitality. We provide the equipment, instruction, and confidence you need to enjoy a memorable day on the water.
